CONTROLS

Xbox controller (standard mapping) or keyboard. Land slowly on a runway with an orange ⚡ beacon to recharge (seaplane: the floating ⚓ Lagoon Dock). Hang glider: no motor, so circle in the faint rising columns (thermals) or ride the windward ridge. Grab the orange ₿ coins (white ones are blimp-only, flown low; just for fun, no real value). Roadster and sky scooter: drive on the wheels, RT fires the air jets (unlimited compressed air) to hover and fly, LT to sink and land. Seaplane: land on any open water. View / ? closes this panel.
