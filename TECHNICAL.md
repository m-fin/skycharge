# SKYCHARGE (working title): browser plane game MVP

Local only. Nothing has been pushed or posted. It uses free tools only: three.js 0.186.1 (copied into `vendor/` from DRIFTHOME, no CDN), esbuild for the single-file build, and headless Chrome with puppeteer-core for tests and stills.

## Run it
- **Easiest:** open `out/PlaneGame_play.html` by double-clicking it. It's one self-contained file that runs from `file://` with no server and no internet. Use Chrome or Edge. Plug in or pair the Xbox controller, then press any button so the browser detects it.
- **From source:** ES modules need http, so start a server with `python3 -m http.server 8765` in this folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/index.html`.
- **Rebuild the single file:** `node build.mjs` writes `out/PlaneGame_play.html` (about 4.7 MB with the music). It uses `ESBUILD` if set, else the old npx-cache esbuild, else the copy in `/workspace/games/xbox-sideload/tools` (read only). `NO_MUSIC=1 node build.mjs` builds without music (about 0.8 MB).
- **URL options:** `?seed=N` builds a different island. `&manual=1` turns off the render loop so `window.pg` can drive it. `&w=1280&h=720` sets a fixed canvas size. `&hud=0` hides the HUD.

## How to play
Take off from Home Field. Fly over the island (hills, beaches, trees, wind turbines and a lighthouse). Keep an eye on the battery. The orange ⚡ beacon beams mark the 5 charging stations: Home Field, Ridge Skycharger, North Cove Solar, Lagoon Pad and Harbor Field. The top-center arrow and the minimap point to the nearest one.
- **Battery:** drains with time and throttle. Below 20% you get a flashing LOW BATTERY warning. At 0% the motor has no thrust, so the plane glides and the blimp slowly sinks.
- **Charging:** land on a station runway and slow below about 20 km/h, or stop there, to charge at about 9%/s. In the blimp, sink down onto the ⚡ pad and hold still.
- **Crashes:** hitting the ground hard, landing in the water, or flying into terrain causes a crash. You respawn at the nearest station after about 2.4 s, or right away with A or R. Gentle, level touchdowns are allowed anywhere on land, and you can taxi on the ground.
- **Cabin chill (Y / C):** puts you in a cozy cabin with a big window, a lamp, string lights, a mug and a battery gauge. The autopilot holds a level cruise, climbs over terrain ahead, and turns back toward the island when you're far out. Parked on the ground, it just sits, which is a nice view while charging. The right stick or arrow keys look around. The left stick, either trigger, the bumpers or any flight key gives you back control.
- **Blimp (X / V):** slow and floaty. It holds its altitude with neutral buoyancy, bobs gently, turns with smoothed yaw, and can't stall. If you switch to the plane while the blimp is more than 50 m up, the plane starts in the air. Lower than that, it's placed at the nearest station.
- **Vehicles (X / V cycles plane → blimp → hang glider → seaplane → plane).** The HUD shows the vehicle name, and the choice is saved (`lastVehicle` accepts `plane`, `blimp`, `glider`, `seaplane`; anything else loads as the plane).
- **Hang glider:** no motor and no battery (the HUD says "no motor" / "no battery" and hides both bars; charging stations don't apply). It flies about 30–55 km/h (trim about 41 km/h) and sinks about 1 m/s in still air. The left stick pitches and banks; throttle does nothing. Hands off, it settles back into its trim glide. It starts on the **Summit Launch** (a small ramp and windsock on the highest summit) already moving. **Rising air:** 6 thermals (3 beside wind turbines, 3 over warm open fields), each with a core of about +3 m/s, drawn as faint translucent columns with slowly spiralling motes (shown only while you fly the glider, also as rings on the minimap). The top arrow points to the nearest thermal. Circle inside one at about 30° of bank to climb about 1.5 m/s. There's also **ridge lift** on the west-facing (windward) slopes of the highest hills, from a steady westerly. The VSI is the 0.5 s smoothed climb rate, and the same number drives Sonic Rift's vario (`setLift`), scaled so circling in a strong thermal reads about 1. A gentle, slow, level touchdown on land counts as landed (press A / R to relaunch); a hard landing, water or a tree is a crash, and you respawn at the launch.
- **Seaplane:** the e-plane's flight model, but heavier and slower (top speed about 155 km/h, stall about 90 km/h), with twin floats. It starts floating at the **Lagoon Dock**, a floating pontoon with a ⚡ charger off the beach below Lagoon Pad. Touch the water gently (sinking slower than 3 m/s, under 120 km/h, wings and nose roughly level) and it floats: it bobs, taxis with water drag, and can take off again (pull back above about 100 km/h). Slow down below about 20 km/h inside the orange ring at the dock to charge (same rate and upgrades as the stations). A hard water hit, any ground contact (including runways) or a tree is a crash, and you respawn at the dock. Running slowly into the shore just stops you. Cabin chill works as in the plane.

## Controls
| Action | Xbox controller (standard mapping, deadzone 0.15) | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Plane / seaplane / glider pitch / roll | Left stick | W / S pitch (W = nose down), A / D roll |
| Blimp forward / turn | Left stick | W / S forward / back, A / D turn |
| Throttle up (blimp: rise) | RT (7) | Shift |
| Throttle down + brake/airbrake (blimp: sink) | LT (6) | Ctrl or Space |
| Rudder / yaw | LB / RB (4 / 5) | Q / E |
| Camera look | Right stick | Arrow keys |
| Cabin chill | Y (3) | C |
| Switch vehicle (plane → blimp → glider → seaplane) | X (2) | V |
| Respawn (nearest station; glider: Summit Launch; seaplane: Lagoon Dock) | A (0) | R |
| **Start the mission offered on this pad** | **B (1)** | **Enter** |
| Pause menu | Menu (9) | P |
| Pause menu: move / select / back / adjust volume | D-pad ↑↓ (12/13) / A / B / D-pad ←→ (14/15) | ↑↓ / Enter / Esc / ←→ (mouse clicks work too) |
| Controls panel | View (8) | ? or H (Esc closes) |

A message appears when a controller connects or disconnects. Throttle is a setting: RT and LT raise and lower it, and it stays where you leave it. Tip: Ctrl+W closes a browser tab, so use Space to brake.

## Coins, missions, upgrades, saving, audio
- **Coins (59 in total):** 34 route coins (2 🪙 each) floating along the legs between stations, 13 landmark coins (3 🪙: a ring around the lighthouse lamp and one beside each turbine hub), and 12 big white **blimp-only** coins (5 🪙) about 15 m above open ground. A pickup gives an orange screen flash, a "+N 🪙" popup and Sonic Rift's coin sound. **Each coin is collected once and saved** (the HUD shows `x/59 found`). I chose this over respawning coins because it makes a collection hunt that you finish across sessions; repeatable mission rewards keep coins coming in for upgrades.
- **Missions (start one by parking on its station: a plane stopped on the runway, or a blimp settled on the ⚡ pad, then press B / Enter).** A prompt appears at the bottom of the screen, and the HUD shows the objective line plus a target arrow and a white minimap dot. The first completion pays the full reward and repeats pay 25%. Best times are saved. Crashing, respawning or switching vehicle fails a mission, and you can abandon one from the pause menu.
  1. **Ring Run** (plane, Home Field, +60): 8 rings in order within 2:30.
  2. **Express Delivery** (plane, Ridge Skycharger → Lagoon Pad, +80): land and stop at Lagoon Pad within 2:47 (about 3.7 km).
  3. **Island Sightseeing** (blimp, Harbor Field, +100): fly past wind turbine 1, the lighthouse and wind turbine 2, then settle back on the Harbor Field pad. No timer, but the best time is kept.
  4. **Eco Flight** (plane, North Cove Solar → Harbor Field, +120): needs 95% to start, allows no charging stops, and you must land with ≥ 70% battery. A simulated careful flight reached 450 m from Harbor Field with about 76%, so this is tight but fair.
- **Upgrades (pause menu):** Battery pack, 150 🪙 (−25% drain). Fast charger, 120 🪙 (1.75× charging).
- **Saving:** localStorage key `skycharge.save.v1`, which works from `file://`. It stores the wallet, collected coin ids, completed missions, run counts, best times, last vehicle, upgrades and settings (invert pitch, speed units, sound on/off, volume). It autosaves on every coin, mission result, vehicle switch, upgrade and setting change. **Reset progress** in the pause menu asks for confirmation first ("No, keep" / "Yes, erase everything").
- **Audio:** Sonic Rift's `audio/sfx.js` is bundled into the single-file build. The AudioContext is created on the first key, click or controller button press. Every frame the game calls `setVehicle`, `setThrottle`, `setSpeed`, `setCharging(on, battery)`, `setCabin` and `setPaused`. It calls `coin()`, `missionComplete()`, `crash()` and `lowBattery()` on those events, and autoCues handles the chill, switch and full-charge sounds. Sonic Rift's newer hooks are guarded with `typeof` checks. The HUD **VSI** row shows climb and sink: vertical speed smoothed over about 0.5 s and scaled so +5 m/s reads as full. `syncAudio()` passes that same smoothed value to `setLift` only when the vehicle is a glider. The game has no glider yet, so plane and blimp send 0 and the vario stays silent. `setOnWater(hullOnWater())` runs every frame and is true only while a seaplane hull is touching the water. There's no seaplane, and touching water in the plane is a crash, so it's always false today; `setSpeed` handles the hiss fade. `applyAudioSettings()` calls `setVario(on)` from the saved **Vario tone** setting (pause menu, default on). `setVehicle` only receives `plane` or `blimp`. The music loops aren't rendered yet. `loadMusic` only runs over http and is a silent no-op when the files are missing; it's skipped on `file://`, where browsers block fetch.

## Music
Sonic Rift's two loops (`audio/music/out/cabin_chill_loop.*`, `flight_theme_loop.*`, 128.000 s = 5,644,800 samples, mono 44.1 kHz, seamless) play under the game: the flight theme in flight and the cabin loop in cabin chill (sfx.js crossfades them). They start after the first key, click or controller button, like the rest of the audio.
- **Single file (`file://`):** `build.mjs` embeds both loops as base64 **MP3** in a small `<script>` (`window.__SKYCHARGE_MUSIC`) ahead of the game bundle. The game decodes them with `decodeAudioData` (no fetch), then drops the base64 text. MP3 was chosen because every browser decodes it, and it's only about 1.19× the OGG size. Embedding both formats would have pushed the file past 8 MB.
- **The MP3s** (`cabin_chill_loop.mp3`, `flight_theme_loop.mp3`, 1.47 MB each) are encoded by `build.mjs` with ffmpeg from the 24-bit WAV masters in `audio/music/out/wav/`: mono 44.1 kHz, CBR 96 kbps, with a LAME gapless header. They're re-encoded only when missing or older than the WAVs. Chrome decodes them to exactly 5,644,800 samples, sample-aligned with the WAV, so the loop stays seamless. (The 192 kbps `previews/` MP3s would have made the file about 9 MB.) If there's no ffmpeg and no MP3, the OGGs are embedded instead.
- **Served over http / separate files (index.html, the Xbox package):** the loader tries `audio/music/out/<loop>.ogg` first and then `<loop>.mp3`. If the browser's `canPlayType` says it can't play Ogg Vorbis, it tries the MP3 first.
- **Fallback:** anything that fails to load or decode is skipped quietly, so the game just plays without music. `pg.music` reports `{ cabin, flight, source }`.
- **Codecs:** the OGGs are **Ogg Vorbis** (q5, about 96 kbps), not Opus. Chrome, Edge (Chromium) and Firefox decode Vorbis. Safari only gained Ogg Vorbis in recent versions, and legacy EdgeHTML (Xbox UWP) doesn't support Ogg, so those use the MP3. MP3 decoding and the seamless loop were verified only in headless Chrome. Gapless MP3 trimming on Safari and EdgeHTML is untested, so a tiny click at the loop point is possible there.
- **Memory:** the decoded loops take about 45–50 MB of RAM (32-bit float, mono, at the device's sample rate).

## Not built yet / known gaps
jobs beyond the 4 missions; day/night; the flying car and boat modes; Xbox and Store packaging. There's no on-screen mission-start marker beyond the HUD prompt and the menu waypoint. Nobody has tried a real Xbox controller or heard the audio yet: the tests use a simulated pad and a mocked AudioContext. **Glider and seaplane limits:** they have no missions or coins of their own yet (they collect route and landmark coins, never blimp-only ones). The glider has no cabin chill or autopilot; the wind is a fixed westerly that only drives ridge lift (it doesn't drift the glider or the thermals); there's no wind-turbine collision for any vehicle. The seaplane can't land on runways (that's a crash, by design), and it can't use the land stations. The "lagoon" is the sheltered water off the beach below Lagoon Pad; I didn't reshape the terrain to carve a closed lagoon. Thermal and dock positions come from the island seed. Nobody has flown either one with a real controller or heard their sounds yet. Per the audio README, a gamepad press may not count as a "user activation" in Edge, so sound might not start until a key or click.
